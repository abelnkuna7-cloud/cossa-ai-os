# Cossa AI Production Charter

## Product mission

Cossa AI is the owned AI Business Operating System of Cossa Nexus Holdings. It
must help the group operate, generate revenue and grow first. After it proves
measurable value internally, it will be offered to South African and African
SMEs.

Growth is not being deleted. `growth.cossanexusholdings.co.za` is the production
foundation being upgraded to include Cossa AI, Cossa Growth AI and the shared
intelligence services used across the group.

## Owned production stack

- GitHub is the source-code source of truth.
- Vercel deploys the application.
- Supabase project `nptyyzyokzgnwnyteeyi` stores production authentication,
  business data, knowledge and memory.
- Cloudflare provides DNS, security, edge routing, rate limiting and protected
  backend/integration functions.
- Groq is the primary AI inference provider.
- Base44 remains unchanged as a prototype, feature inventory and workflow
  reference only.

Cossa Nexus Holdings must own the code, data, architecture, integrations and
deployment. Production must not depend on builder credits or a closed builder.

## Ecosystem boundaries

- The main Cossa Nexus Holdings website provides public trust, service
  information and lead capture.
- Growth/Cossa Growth AI owns marketing, CRM, leads, sales, follow-ups and
  conversion intelligence.
- Cossa Workforce owns AI employees, missions, handoffs, approvals, execution
  and internal operations.
- NexDocs at `nexdocs.cossanexusholdings.co.za` is the official document engine
  for quotations, proposals, contracts and controlled business documents.
- Cossa Store owns commerce, products, orders, dropshipping, print-on-demand and
  affiliate workflows.
- Cossa Nexus Construction, Cossa Facility Services and Cossa Store are the
  first commercial focus.
- Cossa Tech, Cossa Logistics and Cossa Cuisine remain first-class business
  units in the shared architecture.
- Public website chatbot conversations and internal Cossa AI conversations are
  separate data domains, linked only through retained IDs and approved
  workflows.

## Intelligence rules

- All AI employees use one shared Nexus Brain.
- Company facts come only from approved, versioned and verified knowledge.
- AI employees must never invent company, customer, revenue or operational
  facts.
- Important outputs retain evidence, source references, record IDs, run IDs and
  audit events.
- High-risk, external, financial, legal or irreversible actions require human
  approval.
- Employee handoffs retain the original mission and relevant entity IDs.
- Demo data and hard-coded business results may not be presented as live data.
- Existing production data is preserved and extended; duplicate CRM systems are
  not created.

## Business-value gate

Every production feature must achieve at least one of these outcomes:

1. Generate revenue.
2. Bring or convert customers.
3. Grow the company or a subsidiary.
4. Save time.
5. Reduce operating costs.
6. Improve customer experience.
7. Improve decisions or execution.
8. Reduce business, compliance or security risk.

Features that satisfy none of these outcomes are deprioritised.

## Delivery rule

Build each shared capability once, connect it across the ecosystem through
stable IDs and APIs, verify it with real data, and only then label it live.
