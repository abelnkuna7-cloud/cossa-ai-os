# Cossa Store Digital Product Intelligence V1

Additive upgrade. Existing product types, catalogue rows, deliverables, publication controls and workforce employees remain intact.

## Ownership
- Product Intelligence Analyst: subtype classification, evidence-backed metadata, package/readiness analysis, merchandising and pricing research inputs.
- Store Operations Manager: workflow orchestration, catalogue readiness, specialist handoffs and publication holds.
- Content/SEO/Creative workers remain specialist contributors.
- CEO retains approval over publication and consequential commercial actions.

## Digital subtypes
The existing `digital` product type gains a second-level subtype: eBook/storybook, guide/manual, online course, video course, software, SaaS/digital access, template, document pack, workbook, printable, audio/audiobook, digital media, AI prompt pack, toolkit/bundle and other.

## Evidence rule
AI may infer a proposed subtype from product name, category, description and filenames. Inference is a suggestion, not verified fact. ISBN, author/creator, publisher, page count, licence, duration, course credentials, prices and other factual metadata must never be invented.

## Readiness
Readiness combines existing publication preflight with subtype-specific metadata and digital deliverable checks. Unsaved form values must be labelled as unsaved rather than reported as absent from the database.

## Safety
No automatic publication. No supplier order. No payment. No fabricated stock, demand, pricing or product facts. Existing production data is preserved.
