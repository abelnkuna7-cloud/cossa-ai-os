# GROWTH User Guide

## Cossa Nexus Holdings Business Growth Intelligence Platform

GROWTH is the central business operating platform for Cossa Nexus Holdings.

It is designed to help the company organise, operate, grow and coordinate multiple businesses from one system without mixing unrelated tools, employees or workflows.

GROWTH combines:

- Business workspaces
- AI employees
- AI CEO coordination
- Marketing and growth tools
- Sales and CRM tools
- Operations
- Workflows and automation
- Documents
- Integrations
- Business intelligence
- Owner-controlled approvals

The goal is simple:

> Go to the business you want to work on, then access the tools, AI employees, workflows and information relevant to that business.

---

# 1. Main Structure of GROWTH

GROWTH is organised into several layers.

## Company

This is the group-wide level for Cossa Nexus Holdings.

Use it when you want to manage the whole company rather than one specific subsidiary.

Main areas include:

### Command Center

Use the Command Center for:

- Company overview
- Business priorities
- Business health
- Opportunities
- Important alerts
- Group-wide operating information
- Executive decisions

The Command Center answers:

> What is happening across the company?

---

### AI Company

The AI Company contains the group-wide AI workforce.

Use it for:

- AI departments
- AI employees
- AI CEO
- Workforce activity
- Employee responsibilities
- Missions
- Workflows
- Execution monitoring
- Owner approvals

The AI Company answers:

> Which AI employee or department should handle this work?

# 2. Business Workspaces

Each workspace should contain:

# 10. AI CEO

The AI CEO is used when you know the outcome you want but do not want to decide which employee should do each step.

Examples:

- Create a Facebook campaign for Cossa Facility Services.
- Find product opportunities for Cossa Store.
- Prepare a construction marketing campaign.
- Audit our website and prepare SEO improvements.
- Build a sales follow-up plan.
- Prepare a complete business-growth campaign.

The AI CEO should:

1. Understand the objective.
2. Identify the correct business.
3. Identify the correct department.
4. Identify the correct AI employees.
5. Coordinate the work.
6. Pass work between employees.
7. Escalate only genuine owner decisions.
8. Save usable outputs.

The AI CEO should not invent completed external actions.

# 13. Owner Approval Rules

GROWTH should perform normal internal business work without constantly asking the owner for permission.

Normal internal work includes:

- Research
- Analysis
- Drafting
- Planning
- Content creation
- Marketing drafts
- Internal recommendations
- Visual briefs
- Workflow planning
- Employee handoffs
- Reports

Owner approval is required for high-risk actions.

Examples include:

- Spending money
- Advertising budget changes
- Supplier orders
- Contracts
- Legal commitments
- Signatures
- Binding commercial agreements
- Credentials
- Password changes
- Destructive actions
- Irreversible account changes
- Sensitive external communication

Missing integrations must be reported.

They must never be simulated.

---

# 14. Integrations

Integrations connect GROWTH to external systems.

Examples can include:

- Social-media accounts
- Email
- Analytics
- CRM systems
- Websites
- Publishing systems
- Business tools
- External APIs

An AI employee should never claim it published, sent, bought, changed or updated something unless an authorised integration actually performed that action.

---

# 15. Business Separation Rule

Every task should belong to a business, department or group-wide function.

Examples:

Product sourcing belongs primarily to Cossa Store.

Website development belongs primarily to Cossa Tech.

Construction quotations belong to Cossa Construction.

Cleaning quotations belong to Facility Services.

Professional document creation belongs to NexDocs.

Group-wide strategy belongs to the Company or AI CEO.

Marketing supports all businesses but should always know which business it is promoting.

Sales supports all businesses but should always know which business owns the lead or customer.

# 16. Recommended Daily Use

A simple daily operating method is:

## Step 1 — Open Command Center

Check:

- Priorities
- Alerts
- Opportunities
- Current activity
- Business performance

## Step 2 — Choose the business

Open:

- Cossa Store
- Cossa Tech
- Cossa Construction
- Facility Services
- NexDocs

## Step 3 — Choose the required capability

Use the relevant:

- AI employee
- Marketing tool
- Sales tool
- Workflow
- Document tool
- Operations tool

## Step 4 — Use AI CEO for complex work

If multiple employees or departments are required, delegate the outcome to AI CEO.

## Step 5 — Review outputs

Check:

- Work completed
- Missing information
- Recommendations
- Required approvals
- Next actions

## Step 6 — Approve only high-risk actions

Do not create unnecessary approval bottlenecks.

# 17. GROWTH Operating Principle

GROWTH should reduce searching, confusion and duplicate work.

The intended operating logic is:

Business  
→ Department  
→ Tool or AI Employee  
→ Workflow  
→ Output  
→ Revenue or operational result

The system should answer three questions quickly:

1. Which business am I working on?
2. Which capability do I need?
3. What is the next action?

If the user has to search through unrelated areas to find a tool, the platform organisation should be improved.

---

# 18. Data Integrity Rules

GROWTH must distinguish between:

- Verified information
- User-provided information
- AI recommendations
- Drafts
- Completed internal actions
- Completed external actions

AI employees must not invent:

- Customers
- Suppliers
- Sales
- Prices
- Results
- Account access
- Publications
- Payments
- External actions

If information is unavailable, the system should say what is missing.
